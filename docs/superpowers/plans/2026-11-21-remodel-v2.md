## Decisions

- **Correctness before looks.** Season blockers ship first, on their own PR.
- **One foundation PR, then area PRs.** Tokens, background, buttons and dead CSS land once, so area workers build on it without touching shared files.
- **Visual direction.**
  - Radii are 6, 8, 12 and 16px.
  - Shadows are crisp hairline lifts.
  - No decorative backgrounds, offset shadows or glow.
  - Mono only for code, numbers, timers and scores.
  - At most one eyebrow label per page.
  - Real product UI is the illustration.
- **Scores stay out of 80** (correctness 70, speed 10), shown honestly. Speed points now scale with correctness, so a fast wrong submission can't farm them. Explanation points remain out of the score.
- **No new tracks, integrations or migrations.** If something needs a migration, it waits.
