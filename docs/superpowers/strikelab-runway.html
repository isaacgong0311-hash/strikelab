Operating plan · updated 2026‑09‑14

32 weeks from pilot cohorts to a YC application

StrikeLab gives ambitious high-school clubs a ready-to-run technical-finance lab where students learn by coding real market models and finish with work they can show. The wedge is a founder-supported six-week Quant Foundations Lab; the proof YC needs is repeat cohorts, retained students, and money.

Decision rules

What a weak number means to fix — not a reason to add curriculum.

Low enrolled → activatedFix joining, onboarding, or the first-session experience.
Low week-4 retentionFix pacing, accountability, facilitator support, or the student value proposition.
Strong student use, weak leader reuseReduce instructor burden and improve outcome visibility.
Five clean pilots, zero paid commitmentsChange the buyer, offer, or price before adding curriculum.
<5 pilots after ~100 contacts / 20 leader callsThe segment or pitch isn't urgent or reachable enough — change it.

Stop / go gates

Week 14Continue past two pilots only if…

One of the first two cohorts hits both the activation and week-4 retention targets, or interviews name a specific, testable fix.

Week 24Move to paid conversion only if…

At least four of five leaders want to repeat and the combined cohorts clear the retention threshold.

Any timeStrong outcomes, weak willingness to pay

Test parent-funded advanced cohorts or sponsor-funded access as a new design cycle — don't silently change the model.

Explicitly not doing yet

Off the table until the five-pilot gate clears.

  • Additional VaR, GARCH, Monte Carlo, interview-prep, or broad personal-finance tracks
  • Real-time market data, multi-asset expansion, native mobile/desktop apps, generalized course authoring
  • Google Classroom / Canvas / SIS integrations, district administration, SSO, automated procurement
  • A public talent marketplace, employer dashboard, recruiter search, job board, or sponsor marketplace
  • Sophisticated AI personalization, automated grading, or new generic chatbot features
  • A national competition — reuse the existing challenge surface only if a pilot specifically needs it
