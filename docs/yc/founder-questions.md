# Founder Questions: one sitting, about 30 minutes

Answer in plain words, in your own voice. Short is fine. "I don't know yet" is a valid answer; write it. Your answers go straight into `application-draft.md`, so don't dress anything up.

Each question says where its answer lands.

## A. You alone (10 min)

1. **Where do you live, and where is StrikeLab based?** City and state. *(draft: location; also unlocks the local club list)*
2. **When did you start building StrikeLab, and how many hours a week since?** The repo's first commit is 2026-08-06. If you worked on it before that, say when. *(draft: stage)*
3. **What did you write yourself, and where did you use AI tools?** Be specific. YC asks, and "I built it alone" with no detail is weaker than an honest split. *(draft: how long it took, who built it)*
4. **Why does this exist?** What did you see in your own school or clubs that made you build it? 150 words, no superlatives. *(draft: founder story, plan Y5.5)*
5. **Did you do anything unusual to find users?** Only what really happened. *(draft: unusual user acquisition)*
6. **What have you done that's impressive besides StrikeLab?** Competitions, projects, links. *(draft: achievements)*
7. **What's the hardest technical thing you solved?** Your own words. Candidate: running Python in the browser and self-hosting it so school filters don't break lessons. *(draft: technical insight)*
8. **What convinced you to apply to YC, and how did you hear about it?** *(draft: last question)*
9. **What would make you quit?** *(interview bank)*

## B. You with a parent (10 min)

10. **How old are you, and can you sign contracts?** State it plainly. *(draft: age and legal ability to sign)*
11. **If YC accepted you, what happens to school?** Defer, graduate early, or something else. What do your parents say? Write only what's agreed. *(draft: full-time answer, plan Y5.1)*
12. **Who is your adult signatory** for pilot agreements and, later, incorporation and Stripe? A parent is fine. *(plan Y6.1)*
13. **Does anyone else have equity or IP claims on the code or content?** Friends who helped, a teacher, a competition or school rule about student work. *(draft: legal questions)*
14. **Have you raised or been promised any money?** *(draft: fundraising)*

## C. Decisions for Friday Oct 16 (10 min)

15. **Submit early or wait?** One aggregator lists the Winter 2027 deadline as Nov 2, 2026. I couldn't confirm it from here. Read `timing-options.md`, then pick. *(decision log)*
16. **Which two or three adults would vouch for you?** A teacher, someone who has started a company, a leader who'd talk to YC. Ask them before you name them. *(plan Y5.4)*
17. **Solo: why, and who fills the gaps?** One minute, out loud. *(draft: team)*

## What I can't answer for you

Whether to go full time, whether to add a cofounder, and what to say about school. Those are yours and your parents'.
