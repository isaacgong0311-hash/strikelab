# YC Timing: submit early or wait

**Written:** 2026-10-13. **Status: dates unverified.** ycombinator.com wasn't reachable from the build environment, so nothing below comes from YC itself.

**Before deciding, you open ycombinator.com/apply yourself and read: the current deadline, the current form, and the terms.** It takes five minutes.

## The two options

| | Submit early (Nov 2, if real) | Wait for proof (Apr 2027, per plan) |
|---|---|---|
| Traction in the application | None. No leader-run cohort will have started | Target: 5 cohorts, 50+ activated students, 2 paying |
| Odds | Low, and the application would say so | Higher, if the numbers come in |
| Cost | A few evenings; can't be unsent | Waiting a batch |
| Benefit | Feedback, a dated record, practice at the real form | A reapply is stronger than a first try with nothing |
| Risk | A weak first impression on file | The company slips while waiting |

## My recommendation

**Don't submit a no-traction application as your serious attempt.** But if you want the practice, finish `application-draft.md` anyway; it is already most of the work. Decide at G1 (Oct 16). If the Nov 2 date is real and you still want to try, the plan is: honest about zero cohorts, strongest on the founder and the product, and clear on what you'll prove by spring.

Log the decision in `docs/gtm/decision-log.md`.
