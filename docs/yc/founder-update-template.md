# Monthly Founder Update (template)

**Readiness plan item Y1.4.** Sent on the last Friday of each month to 3–5 advisors and anyone who has helped. It keeps them useful (they know what to help with) and builds the month-by-month record YC asks about. Ten minutes to write; under one minute to read.

---

**Subject:** StrikeLab, [Month YYYY]: [the one-line headline, e.g. "first cohort hit 70% activation"]

Hi all,

**Three numbers** (from the numbers card; same three every month so the trend shows)
- Capstones submitted: [N] (last month [N])
- Activation / week-4 retention: [X%] / [Y%] across [N] cohorts
- Pipeline: [N] leaders contacted, [N] pilots locked, [N] paid or committed

**One thing I learned**
[One specific, surprising thing from a leader or student, ideally a quote you have permission to share. Not a feature list.]

**What went wrong**
[One honest line. Advisors trust updates that include bad news.]

**One ask**
[Something a reader can do in 15 minutes: an introduction to a named kind of person, feedback on one decision, a school contact.]

Thanks,
[Name]

---

**Rules:** no number that isn't on the numbers card; no student names; send it even in a bad month (the bad months are the ones that show how you work).
