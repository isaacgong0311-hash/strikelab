# Season 1 email drafts: learners and potential buyers

**Prepared October 29, 2026. Drafts only; none sent.** Personalize the bracketed text, check the live destination, and log each send in the private CRM. Use these only where the recipient has opted in or there is a relevant one-to-one relationship. Do not email minors directly or imply StrikeLab has measured learning outcomes, customers, or sponsors.

The product offer today: free lessons and a free Season 1, November 30 to December 20. A January intensive is waitlist-only. These drafts deliberately ask prospective payers about their needs before making a sales claim.

**Use only for:** people who opted into product emails. Check the applicable unsubscribe and consent flow before any bulk send. Do not turn this into a cold message to students.

## Follow-up for one-to-one adult outreach

Send once, five to seven days after the first note:

> Hi [name], one follow-up on StrikeLab. If [club/program] isn't a fit, what would have to change for it to be useful? A one-line answer is plenty. Thanks either way.

Stop after that unless they reply. Record the objection in the CRM without adding a student name or personal details to this public repository.
