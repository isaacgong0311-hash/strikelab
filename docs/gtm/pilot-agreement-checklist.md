# Pilot Agreement Checklist

**This is not legal advice and not a substitute for one.** Before a school or club leader signs anything, or before StrikeLab converts a free pilot into a paid contract, have a qualified professional review the actual agreement, privacy policy, terms, and any school data-privacy addendum (DPA). U.S. Department of Education guidance on classroom tools: [Protecting Student Privacy](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/i-want-use-online-tool-or-application-part-my-course-however-i-am-worried-it-violation-ferpa).

Use this list to check that a drafted agreement actually covers the things that matter, not as a template to sign as-is.

## What the agreement should say

- [ ] What data is collected (account info, progress, submitted code, capstone content) and why
- [ ] Who can see a student's work — the student and their instructor only, by default
- [ ] That names, code, rankings, and capstones are **not** public by default, and what "unlisted share link" means if a student opts in
- [ ] How long data is retained, and what happens to it if the pilot ends or the school leaves
- [ ] Who to contact for an access, correction, or deletion request, and the expected turnaround
- [ ] What happens if there's a data incident — who gets notified and how fast
- [ ] The 13+ age boundary and how it's enforced at signup
- [ ] What the leader is responsible for (recruitment, meeting time, school approval, weekly feedback) vs. what StrikeLab provides (platform, guide, support)
- [ ] The six-week duration and what "free pilot" includes — and doesn't
- [ ] The stated post-pilot price ($499/yr school, $199/yr club) and that continuing is the leader's choice, not automatic
- [ ] A named subprocessor list if the platform uses third-party services (hosting, email, analytics, payments) that touch student data

## Before offering this to an actual school (not just an independent club)

- [ ] Confirm whether the school requires a signed DPA before any outside tool touches student data
- [ ] Confirm whether school IT/administration needs to approve the tool, separately from the teacher
- [ ] Have the privacy policy and terms reviewed by counsel, not just written in plain language
- [ ] Decide, with counsel, whether FERPA's "school official" exception applies to this use, or whether parental consent is required

## Not required for an independent, non-school club

An independent club (not run through a school) has a lower bar — but the data-handling commitments above (visibility, retention, deletion, incident response) still apply, because the students are still minors.
