Hi [leader name],

I'm Isaac, and I built StrikeLab: a browser-based platform where students learn quantitative finance by writing the code themselves — deriving Black-Scholes, computing the Greeks, backtesting a strategy — instead of reading slides or playing a stock-picking game.

I'd like to run the **Quant Foundations Lab** with your club this term, free, with me directly supporting the pilot.

**What it is**

A fixed six-week program, one 45–60 minute meeting a week:

1. **Markets and risk** — how markets actually work, and why diversification matters
2. **Options and payoffs** — calls, puts, and no-arbitrage
3. **Pricing** — implement and test the Black-Scholes formula
4. **Risk sensitivities** — the Greeks, divided across students or teams
5. **Research discipline** — backtest a strategy without fooling yourself with look-ahead bias
6. **Capstone** — each student presents a model or strategy, with code and a short defense

Students write real Python in the browser — no setup required. Every lesson auto-grades and tracks progress you can see as the leader.

**What I provide**

- The platform, free, for every student
- A facilitator guide with a script for each week, so you don't need a finance or coding background to run it
- A join link/code so students enroll themselves
- A dashboard showing who's active, who's behind, and who needs help
- Direct support from me — a kickoff call, a 10-minute check-in most weeks, and I'll join a session if useful

**What I need from you**

- The weekly meeting time (in person or virtual — your call)
- You recruiting your own students (10–25 is the sweet spot)
- School approval if your school requires it for outside tools
- 15 minutes at the end for a short feedback conversation

**What students walk away with**

A capstone artifact — real code and a real result — they can put on a resume, a college application, or show a recruiter. Not a certificate for finishing a course; a thing they built.
